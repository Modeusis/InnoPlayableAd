The Prophecy of the Royal Heir

In the ancient kingdom of Eldoria, a dark shadow falls upon the royal castle. The newborn prince, blessed with a magical aura that maintains peace in the realm, has been kidnapped by the sinister Dark Sorcerer Malakar.

As Sir Galen, a brave knight of the royal guard, you were the only one to survive the attack. Now, you must embark on a perilous journey through:

  • 🌲 The Enchanted Forest of Whispers
  • 🏔️ The Dragon's Peak Mountains
  • 💀 The Valley of Lost Souls
  • 🏰 Malakar's Dark Fortress

The fate of the entire kingdom rests on your shoulders. Every moment counts as the baby's magical protection weakens with each passing hour.
